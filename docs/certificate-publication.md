Weekly certificates have a publication switch in the teacher award form and history. New certificates default to private. Only the existing verified weekly-certificate teachers (Sister Saba and Ustadh Mehran) may publish or hide them, including certificates previously saved by either teacher.

Private certificates are available for authorized teacher preview/download, but not in parent/student portals or direct family certificate links. Publishing creates the 150-point Qabila credit and family notification, and attempts the recognition email. Repeating a publish request does not duplicate credits or notifications. Hiding withdraws the credit and removes portal notifications. Publishing again restores one credit and announces the publication again. Already delivered emails cannot be recalled. Publication is manual; there is no automatic two-day release.

The `20261031120000_certificate_publication` migration adds the Mum certificate's original point recipient and, as requested, hides existing weekly certificates, removes their notifications and withdraws only their certificate Qabila credits. Certificate records and unrelated badges/points remain intact. `npm start` now runs `prisma migrate deploy` before starting the app, so the privacy migration is applied during the normal startup. The existing `npm run start:migrate` command also supports this. A hosting command that calls `next start` directly must apply migrations first. The migration cannot be verified against production from a local checkout without a live database connection.

Weekly certificates are excluded from collectible badge galleries and badge download links. An old weekly `/badges/code` URL redirects to the certificate. No additional badge record is created for either type of weekly certificate.

To release a certificate later, open the Mumin or Mum weekly certificate tab, find its entry in the history, check **Public**, and click **Save visibility**. To hide a published certificate, uncheck **Public** and save. The **Hide all weekly certificates** control is also available for later use and requires explicit confirmation.
